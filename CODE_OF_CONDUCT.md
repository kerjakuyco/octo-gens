# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give and accept feedback gracefully, and keep the project welcoming to people of every background and level of experience. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainers privately through the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/kerjakuyco). Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block accounts that break this code, following the enforcement guidelines in the Contributor Covenant.
